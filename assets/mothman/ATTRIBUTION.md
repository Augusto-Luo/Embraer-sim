# Eclipsis Mothman

- Original title: **Kaiju Universe Eclipsis Mothman**
- Author recorded in the source GLB: **Roblox Kaiju Models** — https://sketchfab.com/robloxkaijumodels
- Source recorded in the source GLB: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kaiju-universe-eclipsis-mothman-6c0ebbba86f5400fa7e4927062ca711c
- License recorded in the source GLB: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Supplied by the project owner as `kaiju_universe_eclipsis_mothman.glb` on 2026-10-30. The copied GLB retains the original mesh, material, embedded texture and attribution metadata.

Project changes are applied by `scripts/echo_mothman.gd` and `shaders/echo_mothman.gdshader`: centered coordinates, giant uniform scale, scene orientation, slow procedural glide, night surface response, and a UV/color mask that illuminates the two larger upper wing eye patterns first, followed by the lower pair. All four patterns remain luminous during post-reveal free flight; a continuous bounded glide and moonlit body response keep the creature available for close observation. No source skeleton or animation was present. Local cloud veils reuse the project's existing `assets/ground/near-cloud-512.png`; no new third-party texture or recording is added here.
